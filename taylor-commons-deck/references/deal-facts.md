## 5. HB 21
- Signed 5/28/2025, effective immediately. Properties owned by an HFC outside its home jurisdiction need local
  approval by 12/31/2026 or lose the exemption on 1/1/2027; LIHTC properties excluded (The Real Deal 5/29/25; HB 21
  summaries; annual report says the Taylor Commons abatement sunsets at YE2026).
- City of Fort Worth's 2025 legislative agenda supported requiring local approval for out-of-area HFCs, so approval
  is unlikely.
- Lawsuit: Texas Workforce Housing Coalition and Post WB Apartments v. Bexar Appraisal District, Cameron County,
  filed 9/9/2025; no ruling found (Multifamily Dive). TexSun Q1-26: no further clarity; counsel checking in.
- What stays without relief: rent and income rules, the 99-year ground lease, ~$55K/yr HFC fees, Pecos consent to
  exit.
- Rent caps vs. market (2026 Fort Worth-Arlington MFI $106,700, TDHCA; estimates): one-bed cap ~$1,600 vs. $894
  market; two-bed ~$1,921 vs. $1,129 (Nov-26 box score). Caps do not bind; the cost is fees and compliance.
